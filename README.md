# Panum Articles

A collection of works by P. L. Panum featured in the book, with publication details and links to local PDFs and online sources.

## 1. Yderligere oplysninger til orientering i transfusionsspörgsmaalet

*Further remarks on the question of transfusion.*

**Publication:** Nord Med Ark. (1875), vol. 7, no. 23, pp. 1–28.

- **Online access:** [Wiley Online Library](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.0954-6820.1875.tb01017.x) — paid access.

## 2. Iagttagelser, anstillede under Mæslinge-Epidemien paa Færøerne i Aaret 1846

*Observations made during the epidemic of measles on the Faroe Islands in the year 1846.*

**Publication:** Originally published in 1847.

- **Local PDF (Danish):** [Panum-Measles-Faroe-Islands-Danish.pdf](Panum-Measles-Faroe-Islands-Danish.pdf) — exported from Word.
- **Local PDF (English):** [Panum-Measles-Faroe-Islands-English.pdf](Panum-Measles-Faroe-Islands-English.pdf) — English translation, dated 1924 in the supplied reference.
- **Online access:** [English translation (PDF)](https://s3.amazonaws.com/aspphwebassets/delta-omega/archives/PanumFaroeIslands.pdf) — free access.
- **Print access:** Original edition available to borrow from the Royal Danish Library.
