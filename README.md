# Panum Articles

A collection of works by P. L. Panum featured in the book, with links to local files and online sources.

## 1. Yderligere oplysninger til orientering i transfusionsspörgsmaalet

*Further remarks on the question of transfusion.* Nord Med Ark. 1875;7(23):1–28.

- **Online:** [Wiley Online Library](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.0954-6820.1875.tb01017.x) (paid access).

## 2. Iagttagelser, anstillede under Mæslinge-Epidemien paa Færøerne i Aaret 1846

*Observations Made During the Epidemic of Measles on the Faroe Islands in the Year 1846.* Originally published in 1847.

- **Danish text:** [Panum-Measles-Faroe-Islands-Danish.pdf](Panum-Measles-Faroe-Islands-Danish.pdf) — a PDF exported from Word.
- **English translation:** [Panum-Measles-Faroe-Islands-English.pdf](Panum-Measles-Faroe-Islands-English.pdf) (local file) · [Read the PDF online](https://s3.amazonaws.com/aspphwebassets/delta-omega/archives/PanumFaroeIslands.pdf). The supplied reference dates the translation to 1924.
- **Original printed edition:** Available to borrow from the Royal Danish Library.

## 3. Experimentelle Undersøgelser over Embolierne

*Experimental investigations into embolism.* Ugeskrift for Læger, 9 and 16 January 1864, printed pages 41–48 and 57–65.

- **Danish text:** [Panum-Embolism-Danish.pdf](Panum-Embolism-Danish.pdf) — a 17-page PDF containing both installments of Panum's article.
